

## DNSINK Game Design Doc

At it's core:
DNSINK is a game where you are confined on a pirate ship, and you mess around with friends.

The player sets their own goals, in a broad sense.


## Game structure / details:
- Players start with a large ship, with prefilled hull, rigging and rooms.
- Terraria-sized worlds/blocks.
- Players have no inventory; can only hold 1 item at a time. (eg. wooden-plank, pistol, beer-barrel, cannonball). prevents players from "hoarding" things.
- Each players has a bank-balance.
- Different character-classes to play:


## Character classes:
- Quartermaster: Comes with a spyglass and a pistol. Earns money passively.
- Sharkman: Very good swimmer.
- Monkey: Fast runner, good jumper, and very good climber. 
- Blackbeard: Can craft explosives and special cannons. Has tonnes of HP.
- Harpooner: Can craft a grappling hook and fishing harpoon
etc, more here later.



===========================


## self-goal sandbox approach:
- players earn doubloons doing random shit (fishing, crafting, trading, gambling)
- players build stuff on ship to improve it. Whatever they want
- players choose to take on quests
- players choose when to attack merchant ships
- players choose when to enter islands to buy stuff
- players choose when to summon bosses
- players discover new items and explore new systems

suddenly, the agency is in the hands of the players.
It's a lot easier to balance, and the game can be very open.


## Macro-goals:
- Encouter and raid other pirate ships
- Summon/slay the Kraken
- Discover the "ONE TRUE TREASURE"

Crucially though, the players decide their own pace, and they set their own goals.
Lots of room for fucking around, toying with new things.
